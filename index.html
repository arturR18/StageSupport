How to Use StageText

  1. Enter Your Text: In portrait mode, type or paste your script into the text editor
  2. Customize Settings: Adjust scrolling speed, spacing, theme, and timer as needed
  3. Start Presenting: Rotate to landscape or tap "Full Screen" to begin
  4. Control During Presentation: Use pinch gestures to resize text and two-finger drag to reposition
  5. Exit: Rotate back to portrait or use the close button to return to editing

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I change the text size during presentation?
Use a pinch gesture with two fingers to make the text larger or smaller while in landscape mode.
Can I adjust the scrolling speed?
Yes! In the settings, you can choose between Slow, Normal, and Fast scrolling speeds. You can also adjust the spacing between text repetitions.
How do I dismiss the keyboard?
Tap Done above the editor. You can also dismiss the keyboard by scrolling in Settings.
What's the difference between Tight and Wide spacing?
Tight spacing creates minimal gap between scrolling text repetitions for continuous flow. Wide spacing adds more gap for clearer separation.
Can I use StageText offline?
Text editing, static Full Screen, Countdown, and themes work offline. In versions with subscriptions, a connection to Apple may be needed to load the Scrolling Text subscription, purchase, restore purchases, or refresh subscription status.
What requires a subscription?
In the subscription update, only Scrolling Text requires a monthly subscription. This includes automatic scrolling with speed and spacing controls. Text editing, static Full Screen, Countdown, text resizing, and all themes remain free. The App Store shows the localized price and renewal terms before purchase.
How do I restore or cancel my subscription?
Use Restore Purchases in Settings or on the subscription screen with the Apple Account used to subscribe. Subscribers can open Manage Subscription in Settings, or use Apple Account settings, to cancel. Cancellation keeps access until the paid period ends. If your subscription expires or is refunded, scrolling stops and the free features remain available.

Privacy Policy

Data Collection

Your scripts stay on your device. The app does not use advertising, tracking, or analytics. Apple processes optional subscription purchases. Read the full privacy policy for purchase and support information.

Text Storage

Text stays on your device for the current app session. Copy text you want to keep before closing the app; StageText does not include a saved-script library or cloud sync.
