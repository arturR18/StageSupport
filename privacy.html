Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

StageText displays the text you enter on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your scripts or messages, and the app does not include advertising or analytics services.

Your text and settings

Your text is used on your device for editing, full-screen presentation, scrolling, and countdowns. StageText does not upload your text to us, a cloud service, or an image-generation service. Text and settings are held for the current app session. The app does not currently include a saved-script library or cloud sync; copy any text you want to keep before closing the app.

Subscriptions and Apple

Versions of StageText that offer subscriptions use Apple's App Store and StoreKit to process purchases. The monthly subscription unlocks Scrolling Text, including speed and spacing controls. Text editing, static Full Screen, Countdown, text resizing, and themes remain free.

StoreKit verifies purchase and subscription information on your device to enable scrolling and restore purchases. We do not receive your payment-card details or send your purchase history to a developer-operated server. A connection to Apple may be needed to load products, purchase, restore purchases, or refresh subscription status.

You can manage or cancel through your Apple Account or Manage Subscription in the app. Apple's handling of purchase information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy. Earlier versions without subscriptions do not perform these purchase operations.

Permissions and accounts

StageText does not require a StageText account and does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, or microphone. The app does not include advertisements, tracking, public profiles, or chat.

Children

We do not collect children's scripts or personal information through the app. Optional purchases are handled by Apple and are subject to the purchase controls configured for the Apple Account, such as Ask to Buy.

Support and website

If you email support, we receive the email address and information you choose to include and use it to respond. Avoid sending private scripts or payment details. The support website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process standard request information under GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Changes and contact

We update this page when the app's data practices change. Contact Artur Reshetnikov at stagetext07@gmail.com with questions about StageText or this policy.
